Voice

Just talk.

Hold the mic, say what's on your mind, hear the answer. Atlas runs OpenAI's Whisper model for spookily-accurate transcription, then a fast Llama 3.3 model for the reply.

  • Natural multi-turn memory
  • Pick any voice for the response
  • Conversation history saved automatically

Documents

Your library, but it answers back.

Upload any text-based PDF — research papers, contracts, lecture notes, whatever. Atlas reads it cover to cover and answers your questions in plain English.

  • Up to 25 MB per file
  • Answers grounded in the document, not the internet
  • Multiple docs, one per chat

Study

Paste notes. Get flashcards.

Drop your lecture notes or textbook chapter into Atlas. In seconds you'll have a deck of high-quality flashcards covering the key ideas — questions on one side, concise answers on the other.

  • 8–15 cards generated per deck
  • Flip-through review with smooth animations
  • All decks saved to your account
